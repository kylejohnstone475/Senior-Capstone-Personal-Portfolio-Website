About Me

Enhanced Biography and Career Goals Essay

   My name is Kyle Johnstone and I currently live in Tampa, Florida. I was born in Boca Raton, Florida and grew up in the suburbs of Atlanta, Georgia. I went to Kennesaw State University for some time before moving to Sarasota, Florida. I attended the State College of Florida before transferring to USF and moving to Tampa and I am now in my final semester. I was unsure of what I wanted to do with my career, but at USF, I found an interest and passion for data science. My main areas of interest are data analysis and data visualization, and my Information Science concentration is data science and analytics.

   The core classes of the information science major revolved around the idea of information and explore the concept of information and related ideas. Some of the more important information concepts include information behaviors, information and ethics, information visualization, information handling, and IT components of information. These concepts explore technical details of information within human systems. The data science and analytics concentration classes revolve around statistical and data analysis and visualization, mainly through the use of the R programming language. Just about every class in this concentration uses R in some capacity. R is an ideal programming language for data analysis, manipulation, and visualization. Some examples of things R is capable of that I've learned through my courses are data and text mining, data munging, sentiment analysis, linear modeling, and supervised and unsupervised learning.

   My skills include proficiency with the R programming language, particularly data analysis and visualization, some python and java knowledge, website design using HTML, and I have various Code Academy and DataCamp certifications in python and R. So far, my work experience has been unrelated to my academics, as I currently work in the service industry. While my restaurant experience will not wholly translate to a job in the field of data science, I believe the customer service, social skills, and the ability to multitask I picked up will be useful in any future endeavors. I am currently searching for an internship or job in the field of data science, which will mark the beginning of my career as an information professional. My dream role is to land a research and data analysis position at a major league baseball organization.

   According to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics' Occupational Outlook Handbook, the field of data science is a rapidly growing field. In 2024, there were about 250,000 data science related jobs, and that number is expected to grow by thirty-four percent between 2024 and 2034. This projected growth stems from an increased demand for data-driven decisions. The typical entry-level education required is a bachelor's degree but master's or doctoral degrees are sometimes preferred. The 2024 median pay was an annual salary of $112,590. The profession calls for the ability to collect, categorize, and analyze data that is determined to be useful for a project. Data scientists should be able to make visualizations to present findings and help to make business decisions and recommendations based on data analysis (U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, 2025).

   As an aspiring information professional with a strong interest in data science, I feel that my career could take many different routes that could lead to professions I currently wouldn't know or consider. My main goal is to find a data science occupation in a business, organization, or government that pays well and offers stimulating and interesting work. I previously stated that my dream role is a position at a major league baseball organization doing research and data analysis. I intend to pursue an internship at one of these organizations or a college, but the field is highly competitive and I must think realistically about my future. I believe if I take what I've learned so far and continue to work hard and build my data science knowledge and skills, I can set myself up for a fulfilling career.
